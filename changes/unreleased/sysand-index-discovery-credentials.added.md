- sysand index interoperability: an index location may carry `sysand-index-config.json` (`index_root`, `api_root`; absent means the location is the flat root), may be a URL template with `{path}` or `{path_raw}`, and is validated like sysand (absolute http(s) only, no userinfo); `versions.json` is checked for descending semver order, no build metadata, `sha256:<64-hex>` digests and duplicates, `status: "removed"` is a hard error and `yanked` is excluded from new selections. `-index`/`SYSPI_INDEX` take a comma-separated list, `SYSAND_INDEX` is read in addition, and `SYSAND_DEFAULT_INDEX` (else `https://sysand.com`) is consulted last; `-no-index` disables indexes.
- sysand environment credentials for index documents and `.kpar` downloads: `SYSAND_CRED_<LABEL>=<URL glob>` with `SYSAND_CRED_<LABEL>_BEARER_TOKEN` or `SYSAND_CRED_<LABEL>_BASIC_USER`/`_BASIC_PASS`; a matched URL is requested anonymously first and retried with the credential on a 4xx other than 429, ambiguous globs are warned about and tried in order, a credential never follows a redirect to another host, and netrc remains the fallback. Hints on 401/403 name both mechanisms.
